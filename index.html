Our Vibe

Where the sky meets your soul

Perched above Fier, BOHO is more than a bar — it's a state of mind. Rattan, raw wood, and warm candlelight create an atmosphere as free as the open sky above you.

Every detail is curated with intention: handwoven pendants casting amber glows, cocktails that taste like sunsets feel. Come as you are. Leave wishing you could stay forever.

Why BOHO

Every visit, a ritual

🌿

Bohemian Decor

Hand-woven rattan lamps, terracotta planters, and reclaimed wood whisper wanderlust into every corner.

🍹

Craft Cocktails

Exotic botanicals, house-made syrups, unexpected pairings — every glass tells a story worth savoring slowly.

🌅

Sunset Sessions

Watch the sky melt into gold from our open-air terrace. Every evening is a painting you sit inside.

🎶

Live Music

Chill lo-fi afternoons, sultry jazz evenings, rhythmic DJ nights — the soundtrack shifts, the magic stays.
